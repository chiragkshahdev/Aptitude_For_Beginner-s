# Formula Sheet - HCF & LCM

## Definations
- HCF / GCD: Largest Number That divides all given Numbers.
- LCM: Smallest number that is divisible by all given Numbers.

## Key Formulas
1. HCF(a,b) * LCM(a,b) = a * b [For 2 numbers only]
2. For Fractions:
   HCF = HCF of Numerators / LCM of Denominators
   LCM = LCM of Numerators / HCF of Denominators

## Identification Trick
- "Greatest number that divides..." -> Find HCF
- "Smallest number divisible by..." -> Find LCM 
- Bells ringing together, traffic lights, race -> LCM
- Maximum Size, bucket capacity, tiles -> HCF
