# Aptitude for Beginners - Zero to Hero

If you are new to Aptitude, this repo is for you. No tough words, no confusion. Just step-by-step learning.

### What is Aptitude?

Aptitude is a test to check how fast and correctly you can think and solve problems. It is asked in almost every job interview and competitive exam.

There are 3 main parts:
1.  **Quantitative Aptitude** - Maths (Percentage, Profit-Loss, Time-Speed)
2.  **Logical Reasoning** - Puzzles and brain games
3.  **Verbal Ability** - Basic English

### Who is this for?

- Complete beginners
- 1st / 2nd year college students
- Anyone starting placement preparation

### How to Start? (Follow This Order)

Don't jump randomly. Follow this path:

**Step 1: Quantitative (Start with easy maths)**
- Number System
- Percentage
- Profit and Loss
- Simple Interest
- Average and Ratio

**Step 2: Logical Reasoning**
- Blood Relation
- Direction Sense
- Series (Number & Letter)
- Coding-Decoding

**Step 3: Verbal Ability**


### Golden Tips for Beginners

1.  Don't memorize, understand the logic.
2.  Learn 1 formula and solve 10 questions on it.
3.  Don't use calculator, do it on paper.
4.  It's okay to be slow at first. Speed will come.

> Remember: Everyone was a beginner once. Consistency is more important than talent.
